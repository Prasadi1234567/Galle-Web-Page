History & Cultural Identity

A fusion of colonial heritage, maritime legacy, and vibrant multicultural traditions.

UNESCO Heritage

Galle Fort & Ramparts

Originally built by the Portuguese in 1588 and extensively fortified by the Dutch during the 17th century. It stands as a unique living heritage site blending European architecture with South Asian traditions.

Trade & Maritime

Colonial Trade Hub

Historical crossroads for Arab, Chinese, and European traders. Famous for cinnamon, precious gems, traditional Galle Bobbin Lace (Beeralu Lace), and tortoise-shell crafts.

Multicultural Coexistence

Diverse Community & Living Culture

Home to a rich blend of Sinhalese, Moor, and Burgher heritage, reflected in its historic mosques, churches, Buddhist temples, annual literary festivals, and vibrant street life.
