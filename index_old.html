About the Area

Welcome to the official Geo-Portal of Galle City. This platform showcases the unique geographical, cultural, and historical identity of our community, including historic landmarks like the Galle Fort and beautiful coastal landscapes.

Galle City is a captivating coastal destination in southern Sri Lanka, renowned for its rich history, vibrant culture, and timeless charm. Famous for the UNESCO World Heritage-listed Galle Fort, the city seamlessly blends colonial architecture with a bustling modern lifestyle. From scenic ocean views along the ancient ramparts to its vibrant streets and landmarks, Galle serves as a historic hub of heritage and community life.
